# Am I Invisible?

After mistaking small everyday oversights for proof that nobody notices her, a quiet café regular returns for a forgotten sketchbook—and finds a reason to stop hiding behind it.

Planning target: 3 minutes; preferred range [135.0, 225.0] seconds. Not a rejection rule.

Main-film floor: 5,000 frames at 24 FPS. Frame budgets are planned, not rendered proof.

Character names below identify speakers in the script; they are not on-screen labels.

## 01 · 0.00–17.00s · Rainy café / booth

**Visible action:** Maya sits small in the booth. Leo approaches carrying two cups, notices the empty opposite seat and returns one to the tray. Follow Maya’s eyes to the bare circle where the cup almost touched. Her fingers tighten around her own cup.

**Story change:** The empty chair becomes a concrete image of Maya’s isolation.

**Camera:** Begin on the empty chair across from Maya; low close-up on the second cup, then rack focus to her expression.

*No dialogue.*

**Sound:** Door bell, soft cup contact, rain against the window.

**Score direction:** Sparse two-note felt-piano motif with room between notes.

**Essential screen text:** None.

## 02 · 17.00–38.00s · Café / booth and counter

**Visible action:** Maya sketches Leo adjusting a crooked lamp. She raises a hand to order; a tray clatters behind him and he turns away. She lowers her hand and continues the same line on the page, pressing harder, then tears the lamp drawing loose and slips it under her saucer.

**Story change:** Maya wants contact but keeps choosing the safety of watching.

**Camera:** Close views of pencil, shoulder tension and lifted fingers; show the dropped tray in the same geography so the missed signal has a clear cause.

*No dialogue.*

**Sound:** Pencil drag, tray crash, a brief apology from background ambience without intelligible extra dialogue.

**Score direction:** One motif note is left unresolved; no dramatic villain cue.

**Essential screen text:** None.

## 03 · 38.00–63.00s · Café / window table

**Visible action:** Max struggles through the doorway with a rain-softened paper bag. Maya suppresses a smile and starts to look up at him. His phone vibrates. He looks down, missing her expression. Maya’s smile drains; she turns the page to a sketch of his wet bag.

**Story change:** An ordinary interruption feels personal to Maya.

**Camera:** Medium view that holds both eyelines, then a restrained face close-up. Avoid repeated frontal talking compositions.

*No dialogue.*

**Sound:** Rain gust, paper rustle, a single phone vibration.

**Score direction:** Soft low pad enters under the piano; keep the missed glance small.

**Essential screen text:** None.

## 04 · 63.00–86.00s · Café / doorway

**Visible action:** Wind scatters Max’s loose coins and receipts. Maya crouches, gathers the coins into a dry paper scrap from her sketchbook, and folds it into a simple wrapper with her little wet-bag drawing showing. He accepts it, distracted by his phone, mouths thanks and hurries out. Hold her lifted hand after the door closes.

**Story change:** Maya actively helps instead of only observing.

**Camera:** Floor-level contact shots of knees, fingers and coins; shared two-shot for the handoff; hold her hand for a beat.

*No dialogue.*

**Sound:** Coins rolling, knees against fabric, dry and wet paper textures, door bell.

**Score direction:** Let the score briefly brighten during the handoff, then recede without a comic sting.

**Essential screen text:** None.

## 05 · 86.00–109.00s · Café / booth

**Visible action:** Maya sketches the empty chair and folds the drawing into her pocket. Across the café Leo lifts her lamp drawing and gathers wet napkins beside a bin, partly obscured by his tray; do not actually show him throwing away her drawing. Maya sees the motion and decides what it means. She stands, pulls her scarf free, misses the notebook beneath it and leaves.

**Story change:** A mistaken interpretation pushes Maya to leave.

**Camera:** Show exactly what Maya can and cannot see. Insert the notebook remaining on the seat after she exits.

*No dialogue.*

**Sound:** Chair legs scrape, paper folds, her steps and the door closing.

**Score direction:** The motif thins to a single sustained tone; leave silence after the door.

**Essential screen text:** None.

## 06 · 109.00–132.00s · Street outside café

**Visible action:** Maya searches her bag for her sketchbook. She turns back, stops outside the rainy window and sees her familiar booth empty. Her shoulders drop. She begins to turn away, then notices the little drawing beside the register.

**Story change:** She almost gives up the one thing she came back for.

**Camera:** Stay with her face reflected in glass; shift focus through the glass to the drawing. No magical transparency.

*No dialogue.*

**Sound:** Bag zip, wet pavement, traffic muffled behind rain.

**Score direction:** Use rain and room tone alone for most of this beat.

**Essential screen text:** None.

## 07 · 132.00–160.00s · Café / register

**Visible action:** The lamp drawing is flattened beside the register. Max carefully turns the wrapper so Leo can see the sketch. Leo has placed Maya’s notebook away from the wet counter. Maya enters. Leo lifts it. Max recognizes the artist; Maya meets his eyes and nods.

**Story change:** What looked like indifference is revealed to be incomplete information.

**Camera:** Over Maya’s shoulder for the reveal, then a wide triangle connecting the three people; close-up on the safe dry notebook.

**Leo:** I kept it dry.

**Max:** You made this?

**Sound:** Door bell, paper flattening, notebook settling against a palm.

**Score direction:** The original piano motif returns with one added answering note; no swelling climax.

**Essential screen text:** None.

## 08 · 160.00–186.00s · Café / shared counter

**Visible action:** Leo gestures to an unused chair beside the counter. Maya briefly glances back toward her old booth. She sets down her bag and draws the offered chair toward her. Max moves his coat off the neighboring seat; Leo places a cup where she can reach it.

**Story change:** Maya makes a small voluntary choice to join them.

**Camera:** Hold the pause before her decision. Track the chair’s weight and her seated contact; show hands supporting the cup naturally.

**Maya:** Can I sit here?

**Sound:** Chair scrape, coat rustle, cup and saucer, a relaxed breath.

**Score direction:** Warm but restrained harmony; leave space for the line and chair sound.

**Essential screen text:** None.

## 09 · 186.00–210.00s · Café / counter then original booth

**Visible action:** Maya opens her notebook. Max starts describing the ruined bag with his hands; she rests her pencil and looks up to listen. Leo straightens the lamp, now lighting all three. Return to the original booth and its still-empty opposite chair. Maya is no longer there.

**Story change:** The opening image changes meaning without pretending her life is fixed.

**Camera:** Intimate side view of the listening face, then a quiet wide match to the opening. End on the empty booth with the shared counter visible beyond.

*No dialogue.*

**Sound:** Ordinary café room tone, small friendly indistinct conversation without new intelligible lines.

**Score direction:** Finish the motif simply; fade into room tone rather than a slogan or title card.

**Essential screen text:** None.
