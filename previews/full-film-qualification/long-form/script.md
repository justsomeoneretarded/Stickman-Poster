# Am I Invisible?

After mistaking small everyday oversights for proof that nobody notices her, a quiet café regular returns for a forgotten sketchbook—and finds a reason to stop hiding behind it.

Planning target: 3.5 minutes; preferred range [157.5, 262.5] seconds. Not a rejection rule.
