# Am I Invisible? — 2.5D opening study

[Watch landscape](landscape/opening.mp4) · [Watch portrait](portrait/opening.mp4)

14 seconds, 12 FPS, 168 rendered frames per format. Landscape is 1920×1080;
portrait is natively composed at 1080×1920. No names, captions or title overlays.
Maya waits, notices a passer, raises her hopes, then lowers her hand and gaze.
The camera moves closer after the missed connection. Walking uses planted
stance phases; the cup stays in contact with the table and hand.

These are original simplified drawings using the approved clothing and palette.
The approved character-concept and thumbnail images have not been altered.
Sound is an original synthetic motif, quiet room tone and timed step/bell sketch,
not final composed music. There is no dialogue in this acting study.

This is not the complete film or a finished standalone short. Other settings,
more varied acting and interactions, full story rendering and final sound are
still needed. The automatic report verifies frame count, frame rate and full
decodability; it does not certify emotional impact, visual appeal or sound quality.

Reproduce with `pip install -r requirements-layered.lock`, then
`python -m studio.layered_film --out output/landscape --width 1920 --fps 12`.
For portrait use `--portrait --width 1080`. FFmpeg and libcairo are required.
The manual **Render 2.5D opening study** workflow renders both versions and
uploads video, representative frames, editable first-frame SVG and render reports.
