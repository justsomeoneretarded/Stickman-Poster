# First actual Blender geometry proof

`character-frame.png` was rendered from real geometry by Blender 4.3.2 Cycles,
with CPU denoising, at 640×360 and 8 samples. The unmodified render receipt is
`still-receipt.json`. `cafe-acting-proof.blend` is an editable scene with keyframed
head, eye, hand and cup controls over 192 frames at 24 FPS (8 seconds).

This is an early procedural modeling/animation study. It does not match the
approved character concept closely enough to claim final character approval.
It is not the complete first film, not all three final meshes and not a finished
soundtrack. The frame is actual 3D output, not an AI concept image or image plate.

The source and manual rendering workflow continue development in
`studio/blender/cafe_proof.py` and `Render 3D cafe acting proof`.
